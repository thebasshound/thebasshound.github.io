AI use case risk triage

Before you approve or audit an assistant or an agent, answer eight questions about it. You get a risk tier, the suggested maximum autonomy, minimum controls and audit questions, in a sheet you can copy into your file.

This is this talk's own indicative criterion, based on the rule “the higher the impact, the lower the autonomy”. It does not replace your organization's risk assessment or legal analysis. Your answers stay in your browser: nothing is saved or sent.

Examples with companies, people and figures are fictitious. Any use of AI in audit work must follow your organization's policies. No cookies · Privacy
